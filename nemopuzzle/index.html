Nemo Puzzle: Shikaku — Privacy policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Controller and contact

Mildworks operates Nemo Puzzle: Shikaku. For privacy questions or requests, email plainpocketapp@gmail.com or call 010-4881-3717.

What the app stores on your device

Puzzle progress, completed puzzle IDs, undo and redo history, preferences, and limited counters used to enforce the in-app ad schedule are stored locally on this device. They are not sent to a Mildworks server. The app does not create an account or run its own analytics or advertising profile. Local data is removed if you clear app storage or uninstall the app. You can export a progress backup to a location you choose; the backup contains puzzle progress and settings, but not advertising history. The storage or cloud service you choose for that file is governed by its own terms.

Advertising and consent

The app includes Google Mobile Ads SDK (Legacy) version 25.5.0 and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK version 4.0.0. When an ad request is permitted, Google’s SDK automatically collects and shares the device IP address, user product interactions (such as app launches and taps), diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers (including the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and, where applicable, signed-in account identifiers). Google says this information is used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention and is encrypted in transit using TLS. We do not receive or store puzzle progress from these SDK requests.

UMP checks consent status when the app opens, displays any configured privacy message required for the device’s region, and controls whether the app may request ads. If a privacy-options entry point is required, it appears in Settings. The exact message and available choices depend on the consent configuration in the linked advertising account and the user’s location.

The app displays at most one full-screen ad at an eligible completed-puzzle transition. Ads do not appear while you solve, at app launch or return, after tapping Next, or for hints. If an ad is unavailable, that opportunity is discarded. The app does not provide rewarded ads, banner ads, app-open ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

Third parties

Google provides the advertising and consent SDKs. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Choices and requests

You can turn off ad personalization or reset the Android advertising ID through device settings. Use the in-app Privacy options control when it is shown. Because puzzle progress is stored only on the device, Mildworks cannot retrieve or delete it remotely; remove it by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. Google handles information sent directly through its SDK under Google’s policies and tools.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and older, including teenagers and adults, and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the Play Data safety disclosure will be updated before the changed build is distributed.
